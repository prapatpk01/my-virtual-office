## MODULE 1 — BUSINESS QUALITY ASSESSMENT

### 1A. Revenue Quality Score (0-20)
```
RECURRING REVENUE (0-8):
  8  = SaaS/Subscription ≥ 80% revenue
  6  = Contract backlog ≥ 12M forward cover
  4  = Repeat customers ≥ 60% revenue
  2  = Mix recurring/transactional
  0  = Transactional only (ไม่มี visibility)

REVENUE GROWTH (0-6):
  6  = YoY growth ≥ 30% (sustained 2+ years)
  4  = YoY growth 15-29%
  2  = YoY growth 5-14%
  0  = Growth < 5% หรือ declining

REVENUE VISIBILITY (0-6):
  6  = Backlog/RPO ≥ 2× annual revenue
  4  = Backlog 1-2× annual revenue
  2  = Backlog < 1× annual revenue
  0  = No backlog data
```

### 1B. Moat Assessment (0-20)
```
MOAT TYPE (เลือกที่แข็งที่สุด 1 ข้อ):
  Wide Moat (15-20 pts):
    □ Network effect (platform ที่แข็งขึ้นตาม user)
    □ Cost advantage structural (ต้นทุนต่ำกว่าคู่แข่ง 20%+)
    □ Intangible assets (patent / license / brand dominant)
    □ Switching costs สูงมาก (SAP / defense system)

  Narrow Moat (8-14 pts):
    □ Switching costs ปานกลาง
    □ Scale advantage ใน niche
    □ Local monopoly / regulatory protection

  No Moat (0-7 pts):
    □ Commodity product
    □ Price taker
    □ Technology ที่ถูก disrupt ได้ง่าย

MOAT TREND (ปรับ +/- 3 pts):
  +3 = Moat กว้างขึ้นใน 2 ปีที่ผ่านมา
   0 = Stable
  -3 = Moat แคบลง หรือถูก challenge จากคู่แข่ง
```

### 1C. Management Quality Score (0-10)
```
TRACK RECORD (0-4):
  4 = Beat guidance ≥ 6/8 quarters
  2 = Beat guidance 4-5/8 quarters
  0 = Miss guidance ≥ 4/8 quarters

CAPITAL ALLOCATION (0-4):
  4 = Buyback/dividend + ROIC > WACC consistently
  2 = ROIC > WACC แต่ capital allocation mixed
  0 = ROIC < WACC หรือ excessive dilution

INSIDER ACTIVITY (0-2):
  2 = Insider buying ≥ $1M ใน 12 เดือน [verify Form 4]
  1 = Neutral (ไม่ซื้อไม่ขาย)
  0 = Insider selling > $10M ใน 6 เดือน
```

```
  Rev Beat Rate:    [X]/8 = [X]%   [target ≥ 75%]
  Guidance Pattern: Raise / Maintain / Lower (trend)
```

### 2B. Earnings Quality Flags
```
GREEN FLAGS (high quality earnings):
  ✅ FCF ≥ Net Income (cash backing real profits)
  ✅ Gross margin expanding YoY
  ✅ DSO (Days Sales Outstanding) stable or declining
  ✅ Deferred revenue growing (future earnings locked)

RED FLAGS (earnings manipulation risk):
  🔴 Net Income >> FCF consistently (accrual concern)
  🔴 Gross margin declining while revenue grows
  🔴 DSO expanding (collection problem)
  🔴 Non-GAAP adjustments > 30% of GAAP income
  🔴 Revenue pulled forward (end of quarter spike)
```

### 2C. EPS Quality Score (0-10)
```
  FCF ≥ Net Income:          +3
  Gross margin trend up:     +2
  DSO stable:                +2
  Deferred revenue growing:  +2
  No major non-GAAP abuse:   +1

  RED FLAG deductions:
  Net Income >> FCF:         -3
  Gross margin declining:    -2
  DSO expanding:             -1
  Non-GAAP > 30%:            -2
```
