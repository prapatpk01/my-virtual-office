# V3.2.5 Anti-Whipsaw Re-entry

This release fixes same-direction chase re-entry after a position has just been closed.

## Root cause
The previous engine used only a short symbol time cooldown after most exits. A profitable
or early exit cleared loss-side re-entry state, so the same directional thesis could be
accepted again on a new SMC/FVG signal even when price had simply continued the old leg.

## New thesis-reset gate
After every terminal close the engine persists:
- exit timestamp
- exit direction
- exit price
- exit reason
- final trade PnL

For the same direction during the next 6 hours, a new entry must form a genuinely new
market cycle. Valid reset evidence is one of:
- a 5M EMA8/13 opposite phase followed by a reclaim in the trade direction;
- for non-invalidation exits, a >=0.55 ATR counter-trend pullback that returns near 5M EMA20;
- a fresh 15M BOS followed by a BREAKOUT_RETEST entry.

For early/AI/spike/BE/SL exits, the softer pullback-only reset is disabled; an EMA cycle
or fresh 15M BOS + retest is required.

## No-chase rule
If price has already advanced more than 1.0 ATR beyond the last same-direction exit,
ordinary SMC/EMA/pullback entries are blocked. Only a fresh 15M BOS + breakout retest can
re-enter at the extended price.

This is logic-based and does not increase the normal symbol cooldown.
