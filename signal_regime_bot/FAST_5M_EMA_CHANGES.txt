DUALCORE V1.6 — FAST 5M EMA ENTRY

Default architecture (no extra Railway variables required):
- 4H: Macro Regime / strong-conflict filter
- 1H: Directional Bias
- 15M: Trend context, market structure, support/resistance
- 5M: Actual entry trigger and entry score

Entry timing:
- EMA8 / EMA13 replaces HMA10 / HMA16 on 5M.
- EMA20 is the local trend reference.
- EMA50 is the location / anti-countertrend filter.
- Position early exit remains slower EMA10 / EMA20 for noise resistance.

Default entry values:
- Pullback score: 60
- Same-bar pullback: 66
- Momentum retest: 64
- Strong direct breakout: 62
- Pullback setup window: 4 closed 5M bars
- Momentum retest expiry: 2 closed 5M bars
- 5M ADX minimum: 10
- 5M maximum CHOP: 64
- Pullback extension: 0.95 ATR
- Momentum extension: 1.00 ATR
- Pullback room: 1.00R
- Momentum room: 1.10R
- Minimum actual R:R: 1.10R
- Stop range: 0.45–1.40 ATR

Risk/portfolio rules preserved:
- Risk per trade: 5% of balance (subject to regime size reduction)
- Maximum total positions: 2
- Maximum positions per symbol: 1

Other corrections:
- 5M no longer hard-blocks the HTF bias during a normal pullback.
- Status and logs show EMA8/13 and edge=N/A before a candidate is scored.
- Duplicate detection now keys off the newly closed 5M candle.
- Pullback/breakout setup age uses timestamps, so rolling candle windows and
  Railway restarts do not freeze setup age at zero.
- Opposing-room checks use 15M/1H/4H structure; tiny 5M pivots do not reject
  otherwise valid fast entries.
