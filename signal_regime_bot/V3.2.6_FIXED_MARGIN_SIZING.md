# V3.2.6 Fixed Margin Sizing

- New positions use fixed isolated margin instead of percent-of-balance risk sizing.
- Default margin: 20 USDT per trade.
- Default leverage: 20x.
- Target notional: about 400 USDT per accepted trade before OKX lot-size quantization.
- Regime size_multiplier no longer changes order quantity.
- Entry, SL, TP1, TP2, AI Exit, Anti-Whipsaw, Sleep Mode and OKX reconciliation are unchanged.
- Telegram Entry Signal shows Margin / Leverage / Target Notional instead of Risk %.
- Estimated cash loss at SL varies with stop distance and fees; 20 USDT is margin, not max loss.
