# V3.2.8 Persistent Trade Journal

Adds restart-safe forensic context for `/trade` without changing trading logic.

## New persistent entry journal
`STATE_DIR/trade_entries.jsonl` is appended immediately after a position opens.
It stores symbol/side, open time, entry, SL/TP, setup, trigger, regime/bias,
entry score/threshold, fixed margin, leverage, planned RR and structure room.

## Enriched close journal
`trade_journal.jsonl` now also stores the entry context plus AI Exit details
(score, threshold, confirmations, persistence, adverse R and signals).

## /trade
- Still uses OKX as the authority for average entry/exit, timestamps and net PnL.
- Enriches OKX history using the persistent entry/close journals.
- Shows 15 latest trades in 3 Telegram pages (5 trades each) to avoid Telegram
  message-size truncation.
- Old trades opened before V3.2.8 may still show `—` for setup metadata because
  that context was never persisted historically.

No entry, exit, sizing, anti-whipsaw, Sleep Mode, SL/TP or risk logic was changed.
