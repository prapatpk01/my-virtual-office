DUALCORE Balanced Rewrite — safety fixes

1. Corrected RSI edge handling:
   - one-sided gains => RSI 100
   - one-sided losses => RSI 0
   - flat market => RSI 50
2. Entry WAIT logs now display score=N/A until a candidate is actually scored.
3. Accepted candidates display score/current-threshold (e.g. 72/64).
4. Revalidates actual R:R after the exchange fill.
5. If post-fill stop or R:R is invalid, the bot sends a reduce-only emergency close with retries.
6. If emergency close fails, the live exchange position is kept in local recovery management instead of being left untracked.
7. Risk state persists atomically across restarts (daily PnL, loss streak, cooldown).
8. Entry setup state and duplicate-signal keys persist atomically across restarts.
9. Logs are emitted to stdout so Railway does not label normal INFO logs as stderr errors.

Risk and portfolio requirements are unchanged:
- 5% planned risk per accepted trade
- maximum 2 total open positions
- maximum 1 open position per symbol
