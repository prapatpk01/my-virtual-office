# DUALCORE V2.0 — Active Frequency

## Objective

Increase the number of valid entries without returning to naked EMA-cross trading or narrowing SL/TP. The fee-aware risk model from V1.9 remains active.

## Five entry engines

1. **FAST_PULLBACK** — existing HTF/EMA-zone pullback with reclaim, sweep, micro BOS or trigger break.
2. **MICRO_PULLBACK** — shallow 5M higher-low/lower-high continuation while 15M context remains aligned.
3. **EMA_RECLAIM** — price touches EMA13/EMA20 and reclaims EMA8; a fresh EMA cross is not required.
4. **TREND_CONTINUATION** — EMA5/9 recross after a real pullback in strong/confirmed context.
5. **MOMENTUM / MOMENTUM_RETEST** — major swing or compressed-base breakout with direct/retest logic.

## Frequency controls

- Dynamic threshold discount is permitted only in strong context; transition context is tightened.
- Same engine cannot repeatedly fire inside the same short trend leg:
  - precision assets: 36 closed 5M bars;
  - high-beta assets: 12 closed 5M bars.
- First full SL uses the normal time cooldown.
- A fresh 15M structure lock activates only after two same-direction full SLs within 12 hours.
- The post-SL unlock requires a fresh 15M range break created after the lock.

## Precision-asset protection

BTC, ETH, XAU and XAG use stricter EMA-reclaim rules:

- actual EMA20 touch;
- dominant DMI and ROC in the trade direction;
- local directional edge of at least 45;
- strong/aligned 15M structure;
- EMA-zone trigger break is allowed only in a STRONG regime.

## Risk and target logic retained

- Minimum stop: max of ATR floor, 0.75% price floor and fee/slippage floor.
- Fee-drag gate remains active.
- TP1 remains 1.20R for 50%.
- TP2 remains setup-specific.
- Fee-adjusted runner stop remains active after TP1.
- Minimum actual R:R remains 1.50.
- Risk per trade and max concurrent positions are unchanged.
